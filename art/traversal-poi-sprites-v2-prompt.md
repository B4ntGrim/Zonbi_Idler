# Traversal POI Sprite Atlas Prompt

> A TRUE 16-BIT PIXEL ART SPRITE SHEET for a retro survival game, not 3D, not realistic, not painterly. Exact transparent 4 columns by 4 rows atlas, equal square cells, no borders, no text, 16 separate readable building sprites with a full pixel-art silhouette contained in each cell. Every edge is made of visible hard square pixels; chunky 2–4 pixel clusters, limited 24-color palette per sprite, flat color ramps, no antialiasing, no blur, no photographic textures, no smooth gradients, no 3D render. Consistent 3/4 isometric building view and upper-left light; simple dark pixel outline, small grounded shadow inside cell. The art must look like carefully drawn SNES-era game sprites enlarged with nearest-neighbor pixels. One distinct structure centered with padding in every cell. Order left-to-right top-to-bottom: Police Station (barred windows, awning); Pharmacy (medical cross sign); Grocery Store (striped awning and produce); Gas Station (canopy and pump islands); Laundromat (washer silhouettes in windows); Hardware Store (tools emblem); Library (steps and tall windows); Community Shelter (entrance and sandbag posts); Water Treatment Plant (tanks and pipes); Fire Station (two bay doors); Transit Depot (platform canopy and bus); School (central entrance and classroom windows); Office Building (several floors); Scrapyard (fence, metal stacks and shed); fortified faction outpost (palisade and watch platform); field water station (barrel and small awning). Each site must have a clearly different, instantly recognizable shape. Transparent background. Intended to serve as miniature 40-pixel map markers and enlarged scene props in a retro pixel-art 2D survival game.

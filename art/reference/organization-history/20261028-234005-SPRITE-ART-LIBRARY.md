# Sprite art library

Primary art destination: E:\Zed_Idler\art

- zombies/generated-originals: full-resolution generated bestiary art, grouped by tier, plus the original 12 key poses.
- zombies/animations/grayscales: Phantasm Walker and Surgical Abomination sheets, frames, and GIF previews.
- humans: survivor loadouts, faction characters/NPCs, and action poses.
- animals: mythical creatures, birds, and riding mounts.
- Vehicles: bicycles, cars/vans/trucks, survival motorcycles, sportbikes, and lightcycles.
- weapons: existing weapon art library.
- reference/generated-sprite-sources: original prompts and supporting documentation. Historical source links in these documents may point to previous locations; the import manifest records current destinations.

The existing small zombie sprites remain at zombies/. Generated originals are stored separately beneath that category. Source files are preserved. The timestamped sprite-art-import JSON records source paths, final paths, and verified SHA-256 checksums.

# Prisoner quest (concept)

**Status:** [PARKED] until faction quests exist (`faction-quests-and-poi-density.md`).
**Source:** Sam, Oct 26 late evening (voice), Code Instructions Addendum 11f. Recorded Oct 27.

- `[PARKED]` A later **prisoner quest**. Being captured (for example, caught in disguise in a Concordance POI) can lead to captivity: held prisoner, stripped, belongings stowed, possibly forced labour.
- Must follow **no dead ends**: a way out (escape, ransom or rescue) and a way to recover stowed belongings.
- *Why (Sam, fiction):* sneaking into a faction base in the apocalypse should be brutal. The current rule (stripped of faction gear, thrown out, standing loss; `concordance-introduction-concept.md` CI21–CI23) is the light version.
- `[OPEN]` when capture replaces the light version (every discovery, repeat offences, Hostile standing, or a difficulty mode).

# Affliction icons — Drenched, Chilled, Overheated (WX-01 B3)

Status icons for the stats panel and the Wandering HUD.

**Flagged:** `ZI.MOODLES.Wet` already runs Damp -> Wet -> Soaked -> Drenched, and `ZI.MOODLES.Thirsty`
already exists. If those are reused rather than replaced, this set may need four Wet stages instead
of one Drenched. Confirm before generating.

## Prompt
Retro pixel art status-effect icon in a matched set, chunky readable pixels, flat even lighting,
1px dark outline (#120d0a) around the silhouette, transparent background, single symbol, centred,
nothing cropped. Render ONE of the following, specified per run:

DRENCHED — a simple hunched human silhouette in cold blue-grey with four water droplets falling from
the hem and shoulders, hair plastered flat.
CHILLED — the same hunched silhouette, paler and tinged blue-white, with angular frost crystals
forming at the shoulders and a visible shiver blur at the edges.
OVERHEATED — the same silhouette in flushed orange-red, head tipped back, with three wavering
heat-shimmer lines rising from the shoulders and a single sweat droplet at the temple.

no text, no watermark, no border, no frame, no drop shadow, no gradient background, no
photo-realism, no facial detail, no multiple symbols, no cropping

## Notes for Sam
All three share one silhouette on purpose, so the player reads the colour and the particles rather
than re-reading a new shape each time.

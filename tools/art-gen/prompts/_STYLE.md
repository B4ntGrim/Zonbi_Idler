# Zed Idler house style — paste this into every prompt

Taken from the processing notes in `zombie-sprites.js` and the finished roster, so
generated art lands close to what is already in the game.

- Retro pixel art, chunky readable pixels, hand-placed look. Not smooth vector, not 3D render.
- **Transparent background**, single subject, nothing cropped at the edges.
- 1px dark outline, `#120d0a`, all the way around the silhouette.
- Muted post-apocalyptic palette: olive drab, rust brown, bone, ash grey, dried blood. Colour is
  saved for the one thing that matters (acid green, ember orange, cold blue).
- Lighting is flat and even, as if lit from the front. No dramatic rim light, no cast shadow baked in.
- Characters face **left**, toward the survivor. Items and icons face the viewer.
- Grimy and worn: patched cloth, scavenged straps, stained edges. Nothing looks new or clean.

## Things to say "no" to explicitly
no text, no watermark, no border, no frame, no drop shadow, no gradient background,
no photo-realism, no anime styling, no multiple subjects, no cropping
